Using this kit

  • Signature Blue is the anchor. Deep Navy carries text and depth. Keep Warm Orange for small emphasis moments like a badge or a single call to action.
  • Blue on white and navy on white both clear WCAG AA for body text. Avoid orange on white for anything small, since the lighter tone drops contrast further.
  • Archivo sets headlines, frequently in uppercase for the bolder statements. Inter handles everything a reader spends time with.
  • Selima is the script accent, reserved for hero taglines. It carries the warm, human tone and should never run more than a few words.
  • Archivo and Inter are free Google Fonts standing in for the site's licensed faces. Swap in the exact brand fonts if you have the files.
